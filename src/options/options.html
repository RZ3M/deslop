Filters

Each switch turns a filter on or off on every site. To make one site different, open Per site next to it. Every filter works in any language, and filters that are off cost nothing.

Your rules

Describe anything else you never want to see. Rules match on meaning, in any language: Canada also catches posts about Canadians, Toronto or 加拿大, and Mentions Canada catches passing mentions too. Pick the sites each rule applies on.

    Focus

    List what you want your feed to be about, and Deslop hides posts that aren't about any of it. Turn on Tell X you're not interested in Behaviour too, and every off-topic post also nudges X's For You towards these interests.

      Accounts

      Filters about who posted rather than what they wrote. They're decided in code from the author's profile and never ask the model.

      Reddit porn bots

      Reddit's porn bots are brand-new accounts that hide their history and post harmless-looking questions to get people onto their profile, which links to OnlyFans or similar.

      Deslop reads each author's profile once from your Reddit tab, with your own session, then remembers it for two weeks. Lookups are spaced out, capped at 1,000 profiles a day, and pause whenever Reddit asks Deslop to slow down.

      Countries

      Hide posts from accounts based in these countries or regions. On X this uses the Account based in line from each account's About page; on LinkedIn, the location on the author's profile. Reddit doesn't show where accounts are based.

        Apply on

        X allows about 50 lookups per 15 minutes, so a brand-new feed fills in gradually; each account is cached for 45 days. LinkedIn locations are read from profile pages, at most 300 a day. LinkedIn may count this as a profile view: switch LinkedIn's profile viewing mode to private if you don't want to appear in "Who viewed your profile".

        Model

        Every post is judged by a decision model that answers typed questions with calibrated probabilities: Jev by default, another model on OpenRouter, or one you host yourself.

        Connect your OpenRouter account and Deslop gets a key of its own, named "Deslop", which you can see or revoke in your OpenRouter keys. OpenRouter's page also lets you give that key a credit limit. Or paste a key you already have. The key is stored only in this browser and sent only to openrouter.ai.

        Any server that speaks the System One API, for example Laya (laya-serve, or Unsloth Studio with Settings → API → Decision API turned on), LocalAI, Ollama 0.35+ or SGLang. Posts go only to this address.

        Self-hosted models are free, but strictness is tuned for Jev, and small models such as Laya are much less decisive on Deslop's questions: they may hide little at Balanced and too much at Strict.
