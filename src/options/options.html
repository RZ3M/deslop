Deslop

Hides engagement bait, AI slop, hate, recycled news and posts from countries you pick on X and LinkedIn, in any language. Every post is judged by Jev, a model that answers typed questions with calibrated probabilities.

OpenRouter API key

Stored only in this browser (chrome.storage.local) and sent only to openrouter.ai. Each post costs roughly $0.0001 to check. Get a key

Advanced

Defaults to ~typesafe/jev-latest, which always points at the newest Jev.

What to hide

Every filter works on posts in any language. Only the filters you turn on are checked, so extra filters only cost when they're on.

Behaviour

Focus your feed

List what you want your feed to be about, and Deslop hides posts that aren't about any of it. Turn on Tell X you're not interested too, and every off-topic post also nudges X's For You towards these interests.

    Your own rules

    Describe anything else you never want to see. Rules match on meaning, in any language: Canada also catches posts about Canadians, Toronto or 加拿大, and Mentions Canada catches passing mentions too.

      Block countries

      Hide posts from accounts based in these countries or regions. On X this uses the Account based in line from each account's About page. On LinkedIn it uses the location on the author's profile.
